Frequently Asked Questions

Here are some common questions about ordering from N & K Home Bakery.

How do I place an order?

Simply tap the Order Now button and send us a message on WhatsApp. We'll discuss your order with you.

Can I order a custom cake?

Yes. You can tell us what kind of cake, flavour and design you have in mind. Custom requests can be discussed on WhatsApp.

What flavours are available?

Available flavours can vary. Please message us on WhatsApp for the current options.

How much do the items cost?

Prices depend on the item, size and customization. Contact us on WhatsApp for the current price.

How much notice should I give?

This can depend on the order and customization. Please contact us before placing your order so we can confirm availability.

Do you offer delivery?

Delivery or pickup options can be discussed when you place your order.
